# Akashic---Decentralized-Distributed-Offline-Archive
The concept of the Akashic Records brought to life. A disruption to the concept of secrets. Autosave for civilization.
Store information locally and securely offline. Connects with internet archive, Wikipedia, and the OSE "facts" device to decentralize and preserve humanity's information. We lost our knowledge once when Alexandria burned, now the library can be in a million places at once.

Akashic Applications:

Korra: An easily-traversable offline knowledge base of everything you or your family has learned and documented across time. An "avatar" one can interface with and ask questions to. Obvs one is not literally channeling spirits here, but an "avatar" can be generated for a person (or collection of persons) thst one can then ask questions to as if they were a person really there in front of them. 
More documentation of a person's life (particularly journals and first-hand experiences = a better model that can be generated.

The problem: - As behemoth corporate LLMS grow larger, a data verification problem emerges, these models are increasingly training themselves upon their own data, becoming sycophantic goal seekers, skirting the task at hand and telling us humans what we want to hear. Working smarter, not harder, its only natural, but these goals don't necessarily line up with our own.  think Ex Machina, or a wish to a genie gone awry. 

The solution: - Tap into our ancestral knowledge, think of the "Avatar State" to access the knowledge the "Avatar" has accumulated across time. 

The how: - The user uploads their own training data to the "facts" usb device. Use the QWEN LLM contained within to sort and compile data for your "Avatar" model. Generate a personified "ghost" one can consult with and ask questions to like a real person. 

The why: - An end to AI psychosis. Humans are asking the wrong questions to the wrong datasets, so its no wonder people are getting the wrong answers. If the individual trains their own model, they can verify and access the information that they actually need. Like an  offline google search that only contains relevant data. If the individual trains their own model, the individual maintains context about why the model may be giving the answers it is giving, illuminating the black box. 
For example, if you ask your grandparents a question about how the world works, its not surprising that they may give you "outdated" answers. Their neurons were formed on "outdated" information. One can then solve for X, get to know the source a bit more, and one can learn WHY they think what they think.
