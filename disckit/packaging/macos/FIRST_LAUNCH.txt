DiscKit for macOS (Apple Silicon)
=================================

1. Drag DiscKit into the Applications folder.
2. This app is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks before the first launch:
   - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click DiscKit in Applications -> Open -> Open.
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later: double-click DiscKit, press Done, then open
     System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down and press "Open Anyway".
   You only need to do this once.

DiscKit has three tabs:
  1 Build discs  - split a Kiwix .zim (e.g. full English Wikipedia), a folder or a
                   website into a Text Disc and a Media Disc, with full-text search.
  2 Burn         - burn both discs on your two M-DISC drives at the same time.
  3 Read         - read both discs together in your browser.

Get English Wikipedia: https://download.kiwix.org/zim/wikipedia/
  (newest wikipedia_en_all_maxi_YYYY-MM.zim, about 110 GB)
